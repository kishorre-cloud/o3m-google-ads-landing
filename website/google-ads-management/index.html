Campaigns we manage

Search, Performance Max, YouTube, Display, Remarketing and Shopping, each with a job to do.

We don't run every campaign type for every business. Select one to see when we use it and how we judge it.

When buyers are already looking

Google Search campaigns

The situation
Someone types exactly what they need, so the intent is already there.
Our approach
We build campaigns around the searches that lead to enquiries, keep ad messages in line with the landing page, and review the search terms your ads actually appeared for so budget stops going to irrelevant ones.
How we judge it
Judged on tracked enquiries and their quality, not clicks.

When you want reach beyond Search

Performance Max management

The situation
You want reach across Google, but an automated campaign only optimises toward the signals it is given.
Our approach
We set it up with your real conversion goals, strong ad assets and audience signals, and feed it conversion data that reflects genuine enquiries or sales.
How we judge it
Results are checked against what happens after the click, not the campaign's own reported conversions alone.

When buyers need to know you first

YouTube advertising

The situation
Your product is easier to show than describe, or buyers want to recognise a brand before they enquire.
Our approach
We plan YouTube as part of your wider Google Ads strategy, choosing audiences and creative to introduce the business or to re-engage people who already know it.
How we judge it
Measured on the downstream actions it supports where tracking allows, alongside reach.

Staying visible between searches

Display advertising

The situation
You need to stay visible to the right audience between their searches.
Our approach
We use Display only where it supports the rest of the plan, with audience and placement choices reviewed for quality.
How we judge it
Judged on the enquiries and sales it contributes, not impressions.

When the first visit isn't the decision

Remarketing

The situation
Most buyers compare options before they enquire, especially for considered or B2B purchases.
Our approach
We build audiences from people who visited your site or engaged with your ads, within consent and Google's audience policies, exclude people who have already converted where tracking allows, and match the message to what they showed interest in.
How we judge it
We watch frequency, audience quality and conversions so repetition doesn't waste spend. Remarketing supports new-customer campaigns rather than replacing them.

When every rupee has to come back as sales

Shopping and ecommerce campaigns

The situation
For an online store, Google Ads has to pay for itself in orders.
Our approach
We run Shopping ads and Performance Max to put your products, prices and images in front of people searching to buy, and shift spend toward the products and campaigns that sell.
How we judge it
Measured on orders, revenue and advertising efficiency rather than traffic.

Technology

AdFlux shows which campaigns produce business, not just leads.

AdFlux is O3M's own marketing performance and revenue-operations platform. It brings campaign data together with lead management, CRM activity and sales execution, so our team can see which campaigns lead to real opportunities and where those opportunities are being lost.

  • Campaign performance by cost and return. Cost per lead, cost per acquisition, ROAS and conversion trends across channels and campaigns, so campaigns are compared on what they cost to produce business.
  • What happens after the enquiry. Lead management, CRM adoption, response times and the sales pipeline, so we can show you when follow-up, not the ad, is where business is lost.
  • What happened, why, and what next. AI-assisted analysis helps our team decide the next change, from campaign adjustments to CRM improvements.

AdFlux is designed to work with your CRM and sales pipeline data without replacing your existing tools. Learn more about AdFlux, or ask how it would apply to your account during your consultation.
