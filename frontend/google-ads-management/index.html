Why campaigns underperform

Most lead generation problems don't start in Google Ads.

Campaign performance is often affected by landing pages, measurement, lead qualification and sales follow-up rather than platform settings alone. We look at where each lead is lost — on the page, in the tracking, or after the form is submitted.

Most Common Issues We Uncover During Campaign Reviews

  1. Landing pages that fail to convert buying intent into enquiries
  2. Conversion tracking that measures activity rather than business outcomes
  3. CRM systems unable to connect marketing activity to sales results
  4. Sales follow-up delays reducing conversion potential
  5. Broad keyword targeting attracting non-buying searches
  6. Reporting focused on clicks rather than opportunities
  7. Campaign budgets allocated without lead quality visibility

Display advertising

The situation

Our approach

How we judge it
Judged on the enquiries and sales it contributes, not impressions.

When the first visit isn't the decision

Remarketing

The situation
Most buyers compare options before they enquire, especially for considered or B2B purchases.
Our approach
We build audiences from people who visited your site or engaged with your ads, within consent and Google's audience policies, exclude people who have already converted where tracking allows, and match the message to what they showed interest in.
How we judge it
We watch frequency, audience quality and conversions so repetition doesn't waste spend. Remarketing supports new-customer campaigns rather than replacing them.

When every rupee has to come back as sales

Shopping and ecommerce campaigns

The situation
For an online store, Google Ads has to pay for itself in orders.
Our approach
We run Shopping ads and Performance Max to put your products, prices and images in front of people searching to buy, and shift spend toward the products and campaigns that sell.
How we judge it
Measured on orders, revenue and advertising efficiency rather than traffic.
