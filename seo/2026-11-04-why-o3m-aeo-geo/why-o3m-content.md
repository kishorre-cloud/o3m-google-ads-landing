## Writing pattern

- Each heading is a claim a reader or answer system can quote on its own.
- Each body is 1–2 sentences: what O3M does, why it matters, and the proof (a link or a named source) where one exists.
- Each card is 28–45 words.
- No "best", "data-driven", "end-to-end" or "unlock".
