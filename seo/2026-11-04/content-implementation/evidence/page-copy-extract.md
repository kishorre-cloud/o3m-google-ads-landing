Campaigns we manage

## Search, Performance Max, YouTube and Shopping, each with a job to do.
We don't run every campaign type for every business. Select one to see when we use it and how we judge it.
When buyers are already looking

### Google Search campaigns
The starting point for most businesses, because the searcher has already told Google what they need. We build campaigns around the searches that lead to enquiries, review the search terms your ads actually appeared for, and move budget away from the ones that don't produce business.
When you want reach beyond Search

### Performance Max management
Performance Max decides where your ads appear across Google, so it is only as good as what it learns from. We feed it conversion data that reflects real enquiries or sales, give it strong ad assets and audience signals, and check its results against what happens after the click.
When buyers need to know you first

### YouTube advertising
Useful when a product is easier to show than describe, or when buyers want to recognise a brand before they enquire. We run YouTube as part of your wider Google Ads plan, both to introduce your business and to remarket to people who already know it.
Staying visible between searches

### Display advertising
Places your ads on websites and apps your audience uses beyond Search. We use Display where it supports the rest of the plan, and judge it on the enquiries and sales it contributes, not on impressions.
When the first visit isn't the decision

### Remarketing
Most buyers compare options before they enquire. Remarketing keeps your business in front of people who have already visited your site or engaged with your ads, so a delayed decision doesn't end with a competitor.
When every rupee has to come back as sales

### Shopping and ecommerce campaigns
For online stores, Shopping ads and Performance Max put your products, prices and images in front of people searching to buy. We measure them on orders and revenue rather than traffic, and shift spend toward the products and campaigns that sell.

Technology

## AdFlux shows which campaigns produce business, not just leads.
AdFlux is O3M's own marketing performance and revenue-operations platform. It brings campaign data together with lead management, CRM activity and sales execution, so our team can see which campaigns lead to real opportunities and where those opportunities are being lost.
- Campaign performance by cost and return. Cost per lead, cost per acquisition, ROAS and conversion trends across channels and campaigns, so campaigns are compared on what they cost to produce business.
- What happens after the enquiry. Lead management, CRM adoption, response times and the sales pipeline, so we can show you when follow-up, not the ad, is where business is lost.
- What happened, why, and what next. AI-assisted analysis helps our team decide the next change, from campaign adjustments to CRM improvements.
AdFlux is designed to work with your CRM and sales pipeline data without replacing your existing tools. Learn more about AdFlux, or ask how it would apply to your account during your consultation.

## Questions about Google Ads management

### What does O3M manage in a Google Ads account?
We plan and run your Google Ads (PPC) campaigns across Search, Performance Max, YouTube, Display, Remarketing and Shopping, and set up the conversion tracking, GA4, CRM integration and call tracking behind them. After launch we keep reviewing search terms, keywords, audiences, ad creatives, landing pages, conversion performance and budget allocation.

### How does O3M measure qualified leads?
We agree with you what counts as a good enquiry, then track form and phone enquiries with conversion tracking, enhanced conversions, GA4 and CRM integration. Lead quality and sales follow-up are reviewed alongside the campaigns, so the account is judged on enquiries that move forward rather than on clicks.

### Do you work with B2B and manufacturing companies?
Yes. Manufacturing and industrial marketing is one of O3M's core focus areas. These businesses have long sales cycles, distributor and dealer networks and technical buyers, so lead quality matters as much as lead volume.

### Can O3M manage Google Ads for an ecommerce store?
Yes. For ecommerce brands, D2C businesses and marketplace sellers we use Shopping ads, Performance Max and remarketing, and measure campaigns on sales and revenue rather than traffic.

### Do you only work with businesses in Chennai and Coimbatore?
No. O3M has offices in Chennai and Coimbatore and works with businesses across India and selected international markets.

### How is an agency different from managing Google Ads in-house?
Your team knows your business best. An agency adds people who work on Google Ads every day and keep their certifications current. Many businesses combine the two: your team owns the goals and follow-up, and we run the campaigns and measurement.

### How much should we spend on Google Ads?
It depends on your industry, competition, customer value and growth goals, so we recommend a discovery conversation before suggesting a starting point. You don't need to share a budget to enquire: the spend field in the form is optional.

### Do you manage YouTube ads?
Yes. YouTube campaigns are managed as part of your wider Google Ads strategy, for both awareness and remarketing.
