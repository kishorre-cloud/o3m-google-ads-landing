## Method and limitations

- 4 extra WebSearch queries. WebSearch is **not a localised google.co.in search**: results were used only to find pages, not to rank them.

- Traffic, domain authority, backlinks and rankings are **unavailable**. No metrics were invented.
- Competitors' figures and ratings are their own claims and were not verified.

## Patterns across all six pages

- **Service work is listed as nouns** ("bid management", "conversion tracking"). None explains what happens in a normal week or month. **None describes a search-term review routine or a lead-quality / CRM feedback loop.**
- **Campaign types:** half the pages write tutorials and the rest write one-line benefits. **None says when the agency would or would not use a campaign type**, or how Performance Max and brand Search interact.
- **Process:** 0–9 steps, rarely with deliverables or timings.
- **Reporting:** cadence is promised (weekly or monthly), but no page names the metrics or shows a sample.
- **FAQs** are dominated by definitions and the questions on budget, time to results and platforms. **Nobody answers** account ownership, contract or exit terms, fee model, the first 30 days, B2B lead quality, or control of Performance Max.
- **Quality:** 5 of 6 pages have visible template errors, conflicting numbers or typos. Credible, consistent copy is itself a differentiator.

## Gaps O3M can own (prioritised)

1. **Describe the real working rhythm.** Say what O3M reviews (search terms, lead quality with sales, budget) and what the client sees. No competitor does this. *Needs O3M evidence: the real cadence and report contents.*
2. **B2B / manufacturing and Performance Max management.** No India page owns these intents. Explain when O3M uses Performance Max versus Search, and how it keeps control (asset quality, search-term visibility, protecting brand searches). *Needs O3M evidence: the controls O3M actually applies.*
3. **Proof that holds up.** Link the Google Partners profile. Make the dates explicit (O3M 2010, Boonze 2005). Add case studies once approved. *Needs O3M evidence: case data, a source for the 10,000+ figure.*
4. **Deliverables at each process step.** *Needs O3M evidence: the onboarding timeline.*
5. **Buyer-objection FAQs:** ownership, fee model, first 30 days, Performance Max control, lead-quality reporting, CRM. *Answers need O3M policy.*
6. **A concrete reporting promise:** cadence, format and named metrics (enquiries by quality, cost per qualified lead). *Needs O3M evidence.*
7. **Conversion mechanics.** The redesign already has an on-page form, and GCLID/UTM capture has now been added. A scoped account review offer and WhatsApp are optional; avoid "free audit worth $X" framing.
8. **Named team members with certifications** (none of the six pages has this). *Needs O3M consent.*
9. **Show what AdFlux actually does in the Google Ads workflow,** where competitors only say "AI-powered". *Must match verified capabilities only.*
10. **Copy quality:** no "best / No.1", no "AdWords", consistent numbers, city mentions accurate and limited.

## Clichés to avoid

"Best / No.1 agency in [city]"; "guaranteed results"; "every click counts"; "AI-powered / predictive bidding" without explanation; "data-driven strategies"; "maximise your ROI"; definition blocks ("Search ads are a form of paid advertising…"); unsourced statistics; "transparent reporting" with no cadence; "free audit worth $X"; large client counts with no method; "AdWords"; city-name stuffing; generic multi-service lists.
