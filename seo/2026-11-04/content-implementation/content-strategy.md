# Content strategy: Google Ads Management page

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Page:** local redesign at `website/google-ads-management/index.html`, intended for https://o3mdm.com/solutions/google-ads-management
**Objective order:** organic SEO first, Google Ads landing-page use second, conversion quality over traffic.

## Messaging principle

The page sells a service; it does not teach Google Ads. Every section answers one buyer question with what O3M does, how, and why it matters. There are no definitions of campaign types. Each claim traces to an official O3M source (`evidence/o3m-official-evidence.md`) or is held back for approval (`content-gap-and-approval-list.md`).

## Positioning

**"Google Ads management judged on qualified enquiries and sales, not clicks."** This is O3M's own established angle: the H1 and quick answer on its live page. The competitor analysis found it is the most distinctive angle in the result set, and that no competitor describes a lead-quality or CRM feedback loop.

**Supporting proof, in order of strength:**
1. Tracking and CRM measurement before optimisation (O3M's process).
2. AdFlux, O3M's own platform linking campaign data to leads and sales.
3. Google Premier Partner, shown on Google's Partners profile; *pending sign-off*.
4. A certified team.
5. Heritage: Boonze, 2005; O3M, 2010.
6. 10,000+ accounts; *pending evidence*.

**Missing proof:** case studies, testimonials, numeric results. None exist on any O3M page, so none were written.

## Keyword use rules applied

- The primary theme "Google Ads management services" appears in the title, the H1 ("Google Ads management…"), the hero kicker and the meta description.
- "Google Ads agency" appears in the hero lead, the Why O3M H2 and the meta description, once each.
- "PPC" is used once, as a synonym in the first FAQ, because the Meta and Amazon PPC pages also exist.
- India, Chennai and Coimbatore appear in the hero lead, meta description, Why O3M card, FAQ, credibility strip and footer. There are no city pages.
- Campaign-type names appear as H3s in the services panels. There is no repetition beyond that.
- No keyword-density targets.

## Deliberately not done

- No new city pages, and no Tirupur mention (O3M has no office there).
- No Demand Gen, audit offer, fee model, reporting cadence, offline conversion import or named CRMs: none is verified on O3M sources (approval list).
- No case-study section until verified material exists.
- No FAQPage schema: FAQ rich results are limited to a few authoritative sites, and the brief listed Organization, Service and BreadcrumbList as candidates.
- No BreadcrumbList: the page has no visible breadcrumb.
