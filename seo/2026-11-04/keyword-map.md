## Secondary themes (this URL)

1. **Lead quality and lead generation (O3M differentiator).** Phrases: Google Ads for lead generation, Google Ads lead generation agency India, B2B Google Ads agency, how to improve Google Ads lead quality, offline conversion tracking CRM. SERPs are fragmented (freelancers, small agencies, US blogs). Few India agency pages own this angle. It matches the business-first positioning.
2. **India-wide agency with Chennai and Coimbatore offices.** Phrases: Google Ads agency India, Google Ads agency/management Chennai, Google Ads management/company Coimbatore, PPC agency Chennai, Google Premier Partner Chennai.
3. **Campaign types as service proof.** Performance Max management, Google Shopping ads management, YouTube ads agency India, display and remarketing. Keep as H3 depth on this page. Do not split into new pages without Keyword Planner or GSC demand.
4. **Buyer evaluation questions.** Management cost and fee models in India, agency vs in-house, how to choose a Google Ads agency, Google Ads audit.

## Local-modifier verdict

- Chennai SERP: local agency service pages, a top-20 listicle, advertorials, Sortlist/Clutch directories, and templated city-by-industry pages (owlclaw). Coimbatore SERP: weaker. It is mostly templated neighbourhood pages (webwonderworks, 9 near-identical URLs), one city page with pricing, and job listings.
- Both SERPs reward pages that are clearly about the city. A national page with only a footer address is unlikely to compete on city terms.
- O3M has real offices in both cities, so truthful mentions are legitimate. Recommendation:
  - **Yes, on this page, in limited places:** one intro sentence naming the Chennai and Coimbatore teams and India-wide service; a trust line or H3 under Why Businesses Choose O3M; one FAQ ("Do you only work with businesses in Chennai and Coimbatore?"). Optionally test a title suffix such as "Google Ads Management Agency in India | Chennai & Coimbatore | O3M" and measure in GSC for 6-8 weeks.
  - **No in the H1.** Keep the H1 business-first.
  - **No separate city pages** for Chennai or Coimbatore Google Ads, and no neighbourhood or city-by-industry pages. That is the doorway pattern seen in competitors.
  - The main local lever is off-page: Google Business Profiles for the T. Nagar and Coimbatore offices (category: internet marketing service / advertising agency), plus consistent NAP, plus Clutch, GoodFirms and Semrush Agency Partners profiles. These are where the Chennai PPC SERP pulls directory results.
  - Coimbatore has a manufacturing/textile B2B angle in the SERP. Link to /industries/manufacturing-industrial-marketing from the Coimbatore mention.
